How this works, for now

hiccup is a solo-built, self-hosted tool — there is no payment processor wired into the app yet, so upgrading is a short manual step rather than an instant checkout.

  1. Click a plan above — it opens hiccup's Buy Me a Coffee page.
  2. Enter €20 and tick “Make this monthly” for the monthly plan, or €200 as a one-time amount for the annual plan.
  3. In the message field, include the email address your hiccup account uses — that is how your payment gets matched to your account.
  4. Your account is upgraded by hand, usually within a day. No need to wait around — you will be able to create or join a team as soon as it is done.

How this works

Payment is handled by Stripe on their own checkout page — your card details never reach hiccup. Your account switches to the paid plan as soon as Stripe confirms the payment, usually instantly.

Cancel any time from the billing portal link Stripe emails you with your receipt.

What a paid account unlocks

  • Create a team, or accept an invite to join one.
  • A shared capture library — every team member sees the same uploads and the same diagnosis.
  • Owner and admin roles: invite, promote, suspend or remove members.
  • Everything hiccup already does — analysis, HMR, config guides — stays available on the free plan too. Paid is about working as a team, not a feature paywall on the analyser itself.
