How hiccup handles your data

hiccup analyses SIP and H.323 traces. Those traces routinely contain phone numbers and IP addresses, so this page is specific about what happens to them rather than generic.

Things hiccup deliberately does not do

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising cookies. The only cookie is the one that keeps you signed in, which is why there is no cookie banner — there is nothing to consent to.
  • Your traces are not sent anywhere. Analysis happens on the server you are using. Where an AI feature is available it runs against a language model on that same machine, not a third-party API.
  • Your data is not sold, shared or used to train anything.

Automatic protection of phone numbers

By default hiccup masks caller and callee digits in the analysis it derives from your upload, so a number appears as sip:+3361…00@example.com — still enough to follow a call across legs, without the full number sitting on screen. Credentials in Authorization headers are always removed, and that is not optional.

The file you uploaded is kept exactly as you sent it and is never rewritten — masking your own evidence in place would destroy it. You can turn masking off per upload in Settings.

If you are on a team

A team shares one library. Everyone on your team can see the captures, projects and guides in it, and that is the point of the feature — but it means you should treat anything you upload as visible to your teammates. Deleting your own account does not delete the team's shared library.

How long it is kept

Captures are kept until you delete them, unless a retention period is configured on the instance you are using, in which case older captures are swept automatically. The current setting for this instance is shown in Settings. Your account is kept until you delete it. Sessions expire on their own.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare carries traffic between you and the server and terminates TLS, so it necessarily handles your connection in transit.
  • Google only if you choose "sign in with Google", and only to verify who you are. It is off unless configured.
  • Resend only if email is configured on the instance, and only to deliver notification email.

The server itself is in France.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can:

  • See and take a copy of your data — Settings → Download my data gives you everything, as JSON.
  • Have it erased — Settings → Delete my data removes your captures and library while keeping your account, or Delete my account removes both.
  • Correct it, object to processing, or restrict it — email the address above.
  • Complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French supervisory authority, if you think something here is wrong.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone; there are no backups from which an erased capture could be recovered.
