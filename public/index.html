SIP failure reference

no account needed — short pages on the failures that send people looking for a trace in the first place. what the thing you are staring at means, and what to check.

  • 488 Not Acceptable Here — codec, payload-type or SRTP mismatch in the SDP
  • 408 Request Timeout — Timer B expired at 32 seconds and nothing ever came back
  • 486 Busy Here — a busy phone, admission control, or Q.850 cause 17 from the PSTN
  • one-way audio — the signalling worked and the RTP went somewhere else
  • retransmissions and storms — reading the ladder, and why 200 OK retransmits matter most
