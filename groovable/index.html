Your computer is idle most of the day. Let it groove.

groovable pools the idle CPUs and GPUs of ordinary computers into one network for AI. Step away and yours joins in. Come back and it is yours again, instantly. Share your idle time, earn priority, and everyone on the pool always gets a slice.

How it works Read the principles

A pool, at a glance

The unit of work is a whole request to one model, placed on a machine that can hold it. Not a slice of a model split across the internet: that has been tried, and every token pays a round trip. So a pool is worth exactly what its best card can run, times how many machines are free right now.

How it works

  1. Install one small program

    One file, a tray icon, no ports to open. It only ever makes outbound connections, so it works from behind any router, office firewall or hotel wifi.

  2. Set your rules

    After ten minutes idle. Only on mains power. Never between nine and five. Never above eighty percent of the GPU. The machine is yours, so the rules are yours.

  3. Step away

    Your machine joins the pool and serves requests to models the pool curates. Prompt in, tokens out. Nothing a job sends can run as code on your computer.

  4. Come back

    The moment you touch the keyboard the job stops, in under a second, and re-queues on another machine. You never wait for it.

  5. Earn, then spend

    Credits accrue for work that verifiably completed, never for uptime. Spend them for priority on the big cards. Spend none and you still have a base slice, always.

Two halves

The pool

An engine on every machine, a coordinator that matches each request to a machine with that model already loaded, and a ledger that turns shared time into priority. Anything that speaks the familiar chat-completions API can use it.

The builder

An app builder in the spirit of Lovable, running on your own machine. One file, no account, no cloud. Describe an app; it edits a working template, checks every change with a real compiler and type checker, and shows you the result live. Generated code only ever runs in a sandbox. And when a change needs a bigger model than your card can hold, it borrows one from the pool.

The rules we are designing to

Whole requests
A request runs on one machine that can hold the model. Models are never split across the internet.
Curated models only
Jobs carry prompts, not files. A node runs models the pool has vetted and nothing else.
Pools are trust circles
Whoever runs your request can see it. So you choose who is in your pool: your own machines, your team, your friends. A public pool says so, out loud, before you send anything.
Credits are priority, not money
Earned for verified work. They expire, they cannot be transferred, and they will never be a currency.
A base slice for everyone
Nobody on a pool is ever at zero.
Your machine, your rules
Idle thresholds, power and thermal limits, hours, caps. Preemption is instant and unconditional.
Nothing lingers
Prompts and outputs are gone from a node the moment the job ends.
